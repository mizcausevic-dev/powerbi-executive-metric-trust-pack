Operating workflow

This is the public-safe version of a metric-trust operating loop: inventory, attest, score, and brief.

01 / Inventory

Map reports, semantic models, refresh schedules, owner records, and finance-facing metric definitions.

02 / Attest

Confirm whether the metric owner, data owner, and executive consumer agree on the same definition.

03 / Score

Separate report risk from evidence depth so leadership can see whether a problem is dangerous or merely under-documented.
